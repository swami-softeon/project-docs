Shiperoo - Important Queries

Allocation

order_header.ord_sub_status 10 = waiting for allocation; 20 = all lines fully allocated. A status-10 order can hold allocated units on some lines while other lines are short, blocking other orders. Both queries are read-only and assume no free stock; set the company in the params CTE. Click a link to view the query and copy it.

  • Lists status-10 orders whose allocation can be released and held, so waiting orders can fully allocate on the next run.

  • Same logic, summarised: orders to release and hold, units freed, orders expected to complete.
